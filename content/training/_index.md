We offer hands-on trainings on research data management for RDM multipliers such as data stewards and RDM trainers in the NFDI consortia, and for researchers. Trainings take place online or on site at ZB MED in Cologne. All training materials are openly licensed and free to reuse.

Would you like a training for your consortium, or do you have a topic in mind? Contact us by [email](mailto:rdmtraining4nfdi-orga@lists.nfdi.de).
