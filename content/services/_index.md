      title: Certification in RDM
      text: |
        Would you like to get certified, or is your consortium looking for certified training? We are the Base4NFDI Service exploring how certification in RDM training could be implemented and what options are available. In this phase, we will test and document a certification process with ten NFDI consortia.

        Keep updated by following our [Zenodo Community](https://zenodo.org/communities/rdmt4nfdi/records?q=&l=list&p=1&s=10&sort=newest).

      title: Templates for OER
      text: |
        Would you like to create or refine an RDM training? With a range of partners within the community, we are currently developing OER templates. The templates are intended to support you to create high-quality, reusable, and FAIR-aligned training materials for RDM education across the NFDI. These templates are intended to be available in summer 2027.
